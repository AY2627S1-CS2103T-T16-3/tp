**Employee records, tasks, and leave in one place.**

HR Book is a desktop app for HR managers who prefer typing commands to clicking through forms. Keep employee
contact details, salaries, task assignments, and annual leave records together, and check workforce totals when you
need them. Records are saved locally on your computer.

[**Get started**](UserGuide.html#quick-start) · [**Browse commands**](UserGuide.html#command-summary)

## What you can do

| Your task | How HR Book helps |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Keep employee records up to date | Add and edit contact details, tags, salaries, and annual leave entitlement. |
| Find the right employees | Search names, combine field filters, and sort the displayed list. |
| Track assigned work | Add and list employee tasks, mark them done or pending, and delete them when needed. |
| Record annual leave | Add leave periods, check their current status, and delete leave records. Weekdays count against entitlement. |
| Review the workforce | View employee and tag counts, task totals, and salary totals and averages for all or selected employees. |

## Try your first commands

After installing Java 25 and launching the JAR from a writable folder, enter:

```text
list
summary
help
```

The [User Guide](UserGuide.html) explains how to launch the app, enter commands, and move your saved data to another
computer. No account is needed to use HR Book.
